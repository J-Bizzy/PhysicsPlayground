# Support — Physics Playground

**Contact:** jackson.bizzell@outlook.com

If something is wrong, the most useful thing you can tell me is which lab
you were in and what you were doing with your hands when it happened.

---

## Common questions

### It asked to use my room. What happens if I say no?

Everything still works. What you lose is the part that uses your actual
room: projectiles stop bouncing off your real walls and floor, and
apparatus is no longer hidden behind your furniture. The physics is
identical either way.

If you change your mind, every lab shows a warning triangle in its title
bar with the path: **Settings › Privacy & Security › World Sensing**.

### Where are the controls?

In the room, on the apparatus. Physics Playground deliberately has no
control panels — the window is a title bar. Look for:

- **Plates** standing beside the apparatus. A lit one is a layer that is
  switched on; a dark one with a lit edge is a button.
- **Rails** with a bead you drag, for anything continuous.
- **Wells** — pinch and pull to make a new charge, magnet or pendulum.
- **Rings** — tap an object and its own controls unfold around it.

Each lab's first-run hint names its own, once. If you dismissed them and
want them back: **Settings › Show hints again**.

### How do I move the experiment?

The cross-hair button in the title bar. Drag the cross-hair to put the
apparatus where you actually have space; **tap** it to snap back to the
middle; press the title-bar button again when you are done.

### Can I get my readings out?

Yes. The book button opens the Lab Notebook. Capture records the physics
exactly as it was at that instant, and the whole notebook exports as CSV
or Markdown through the share sheet.

### Is the physics real?

Yes, and that is the point of the app. SI units, real constants, and
exact closed-form solutions wherever they exist — Kepler orbits from
orbital elements, the Foucault sine law, `r = mv/qB`, nodal analysis for
circuits. Where something is deliberately unphysical so you can see it at
all — the magnetic field that bends a visible ball is about a thousand
times the strongest magnet ever built — the app says so, on screen, next
to the number.

### Something is off-screen or out of reach.

Use the cross-hair to move the lab toward you. Several labs also zoom:
pinch with both hands and pull.

### Which devices?

Apple Vision Pro, visionOS 26.0 or later.
