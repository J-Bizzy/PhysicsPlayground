# Support — Physics Playground

If something is wrong, the most useful thing you can tell me is which lab

## Common questions

### It asked to use my room. What happens if I say no?

Everything still works. What you lose is the part that uses your actual
room: projectiles stop bouncing off your real walls and floor, and
apparatus is no longer hidden behind your furniture. The physics is
identical either way.

If you change your mind, every lab shows a warning triangle in its title
bar with the path — **Settings › Privacy & Security › World Sensing** —
and an **Open Settings** button that takes you there.

### Where are the controls?

On the objects first. Physics Playground changes a value by handling the
thing that has it:

- **Wells** — pinch and pull to make a new charge, magnet, pendulum,
  ball, hoop, world or circuit part.
- **Rings** — tap an object and its own controls unfold around it.
  Pull the bead along the ring's track to change the object's value: a
  charge grows, a magnet strengthens, a part's resistance climbs. A
  pendulum's cable pays out from the handle just past its bob.
- **The aim bead** in Projectile Motion — where it points is the aim,
  how far out it stands is the launch speed.

Everything else is behind **More** (the ⋯ beside the lab's name): the
lab's switches and modes, its live numbers, room dimming, Deep space,
Settings and Reset. Deep space is also on the home's More: it stands you
among the real stars on a glass platform instead of in your room, and the
choice is remembered.

### How do I move the experiment?

**More › Move the lab.** A cross-hair appears: drag it to put the
apparatus where you actually have space; **tap** it to snap back to the
middle; switch Move the lab off when you are done.

### Is the physics real?

Yes, and that is the point of the app. SI units, real constants, and
exact closed-form solutions wherever they exist — Kepler orbits from
orbital elements, the exact field of a finite charged plate, `r = mv/qB`,
nodal analysis for circuits. Where something is deliberately unphysical so you can see it at
all — the magnetic field that bends a visible ball is about a thousand
times the strongest magnet ever built — the app says so, on screen, next
to the number.

### Something is off-screen or out of reach.

Use **More › Move the lab** to bring it toward you. Several labs also
zoom: pinch with both hands and pull.

### Which devices?

Apple Vision Pro, visionOS 26.0 or later.
